Untangle Art: Arrow Puzzle

Untangle the arrows. Reveal the picture.

Every board is a picture traced in arrows. Tap an arrow and it slides out the way it points, as long as nothing blocks its path. Clear the whole board to reveal the picture hidden underneath, then add it to your gallery.

  • Crisp, detailed pictures, from gentle starters to truly tricky boards
  • A new Daily Puzzle every day, with a calendar and streaks
  • Calm light and dark looks, no timers, no rush
  • Plays offline, on phones, tablets and Chromebooks
