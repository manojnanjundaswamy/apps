Privacy Policy

Untangle Art: Arrow Puzzle · Effective 18 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Untangle Art: Arrow Puzzle app ("the app") uses, why, and the choices you have. The app is made by Vimora Studio ("we", "us"), an independent developer. Contact: support@manojnanjundaswamy.com.

The short version

  • You don't need an account. We never ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or location.
  • Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may use your device's advertising ID and similar data to show and measure ads, as described below.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), we ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your progress, solved pictures, daily puzzle streaks, hints and settings (such as sound and appearance) in the app's local storage on your device. This information is not sent to us. If you create a backup code in Settings, the code is generated on your device and contains only your game progress; it is shared only if you choose to copy or send it yourself.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and supported by ads, including optional rewarded ads you choose to watch (for example to get a hint or skip a level). Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve, personalise, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID
  • IP address (used to estimate approximate location)
  • Information about your device and the app, and how you interact with ads

This data is collected and handled by Google under its own policies: see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

Consent

In regions where consent is required, the app shows a consent message from Google's User Messaging Platform when you first open it. You can choose whether ads are personalised. You can review or change your choice later from the app's Settings screen. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalised ads.

App analytics and crash reports

To understand which levels are too hard or too easy and to fix problems, the app may use Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics. These collect anonymous usage events (such as "level started" or "level completed"), device type, app version and crash details, linked to a random app-instance identifier rather than to your identity. See Privacy and security in Firebase.

Purchases

If the app offers an optional purchase (for example to remove ads), payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details; we only receive confirmation that the purchase was made so the app can unlock it.

Sharing

If you use the Share button, the app creates a picture card on your device and passes it to the app you choose. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with anyone other than the service providers named in this policy.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Data retention and deletion

Game progress stays on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app, which deletes it. Data held by Google for advertising, analytics and crash reporting is retained according to Google's policies. To ask about or request deletion of any data associated with your use of the app, email support@manojnanjundaswamy.com.

Security

Data sent by the Google services in the app is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we use reputable providers and collect as little as possible.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal information. Because we do not hold an account or profile for you, most requests are best handled through your device's ad settings or by clearing the app's data, but you can always contact us and we will help.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. We will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above.
